# Amazon-India-E-Commerce-Performance-Insights
Developed an Excel-based Amazon India e-commerce performance dashboard using 10,000 order records from January 2024 to August 2026. The dashboard tracks ₹155.79M in sales, ₹33.17M in profit, 10,000 orders, and a 21% profit margin through KPI cards, PivotTables, and charts. It analyzes category-wise sales, profit, and units sold; ranks the top 10 products by revenue; and examines order-status distribution, returns and cancellations by category, payment-method performance, and state-level sales, profit, and order volume. The analysis covers five product categories, 25 products, and 10 shipping states, transforming transaction-level data on pricing, discounts, quantities, fulfillment, payments, and delivery status into insights for product prioritization, regional planning, and operational monitoring.
